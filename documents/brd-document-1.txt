Business Requirements Document (BRD)
A BRD explains why the business needs a project and what high-level goals it must hit.

Project Name
Smart Delivery Window Saver

Business Goals
* Reduce missed grocery deliveries by 30% within three months of launch.
* Lower customer support chat volume regarding failed deliveries by 20%.
* Prevent the spoilage of fresh and frozen food caused by delayed second trips.

Project Scope
* In Scope: Sending text messages or push alerts to customers 60 minutes before delivery, building a live map tracking link, and adding a one-click button to delay the drop-off time by 30 or 60 minutes.
* Out of Scope: Changing delivery addresses after the driver has left the warehouse.

Key Stakeholders
* Operations Team: Manages drivers and delivery routes.
* Customer Support Team: Handles customer complaints and refunds.
* End Users: Grocery shoppers who need their items delivered safely.
